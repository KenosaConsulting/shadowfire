# List of Tor onion services

List of Tor onion services

This is a categorized list of [notable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Notability "Wikipedia:Notability") **[onion services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onion_services "Onion services")** (formerly, **hidden services**)[\[1\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Tor_onion_services#cite_note-1) accessible through the [Tor anonymity network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tor_(anonymity_network) "Tor (anonymity network)"). Defunct services and those accessed by deprecated V2 addresses are marked.

## Archive, Index and Torrent

- [archive.today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archive.today "Archive.today")–A web archiving site, founded in 2012, that saves snapshots on demand[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Tor_onion_services#cite_note-2)
- [Demonoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demonoid "Demonoid")–Torrent[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Tor_onion_services#cite_note-3)
- [Internet Archive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Archive "Internet Archive")–A web archiving site
- [KickassTorrents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KickassTorrents "KickassTorrents")(defunct)–A [BitTorrent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BitTorrent "BitTorrent") index[\[4\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Tor_onion_services#cite_note-4)
- [Sci-Hub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sci-Hub "Sci-Hub")(V2)–Search engine which bypasses [paywalls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paywall "Paywall") to provide free access to scientific and academic research papers and articles.[\[5\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Tor_onion_services#cite_note-5)
- [The Pirate Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pirate_Bay "The Pirate Bay")–A BitTorrent index[\[6\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Tor_onion_services#cite_note-6)[\[7\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Tor_onion_services#cite_note-7)
- [Z-Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z-Library "Z-Library")–Many instances exist.[\[8\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Tor_onion_services#cite_note-8)
